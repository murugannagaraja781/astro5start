🌙 Refund & Cancellation Policy

Last Updated: October 2026
At Astro 5 Star, we strive to deliver transparent, reliable, and high-quality astrological consultation experiences. Please review our policies regarding consultation sessions, wallet transactions, and refund requests.

1. Refund Eligibility & Conditions

Refunds may be considered and approved under the following verified conditions:

  • Technical Malfunctions: Verified audio/video call drops, server disconnection, or inaudible astrologer connection resulting from system failure on Astro 5 Star's infrastructure.
  • Service Unavailability: Inability of the platform to connect you with your requested astrologer while deductions occurred.
  • Duplicate Billing or Payment Gateway Glitches: Where a payment gateway charged your account multiple times for a single recharge transaction.
  • Quality Grievance: Where an astrologer acted inappropriately or violated platform conduct guidelines during a session (subject to quality audit).

2. Cancellation Policy

  • Live Consultations: Consultations once actively commenced and accepted by both user and astrologer are billed per minute and cannot be retroactively cancelled for minutes already utilized.
  • Astrology Reports: Customized horoscope reports or calculations once generated and processed are non-refundable.
  • Failed Connections: If a call fails to connect within the initial connection grace period (e.g. within 50 seconds), no consultation charges are incurred.

3. Refund Processing & Mode

  • Refunds are approved at the discretion of the Astro 5 Star administration following internal session audit.
  • Approved consultation refunds are primarily credited back directly to your Astro 5 Star Wallet Balance for future use.
  • In cases of duplicate payment gateway deductions, refunds will be reversed to the original payment source (bank account / UPI / card) within 5–7 business days per banking settlement cycles.

4. How to Request a Refund

Refund claims must be submitted within 7 days of the affected consultation or transaction:

  • Via Contact Page: Submit an inquiry under "Wallet & Payment Inquiry" on our Contact Page.
  • Via Email: Send your registered phone number, session date/time, and transaction ID to admin@astro5star.com or support@astro5star.com.
  • Via WhatsApp: Contact our support team directly at +91 9080061700.
