🔒 Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 2026
Your Privacy Matters: Astro 5 Star is committed to safeguarding your personal data and astrological information. We do not sell your personal data to third-party advertisers.

This Privacy Policy outlines how Astro 5 Star ("we", "our", or "us", available via https://astro5star.com and the mobile application) collects, uses, stores, and protects your information when you access our services.

1. Information We Collect

We only collect information necessary to provide authentication, astrological calculations, and consultation services:

  • Authentication Information: Your international phone number and dialing code used for OTP (One-Time Password) verification via our SMS gateway provider (MSG91). Optional profile name and avatar picture.
  • Birth & Astrological Details: Date of birth, time of birth, place of birth, geographic coordinates (latitude/longitude), and timezone offset. This data is required solely to compute Vedic Kundli, Rasi/Navamsa planetary positions, and Porutham compatibility.
  • Consultation & Communication Data: Audio and video consultations are transmitted in real time between clients and astrologers using secure WebRTC protocols. Microphone and Camera permissions are requested only during active consultation sessions.
  • Wallet & Transaction Records: Wallet balance, recharge package selections, bonus credits, and transaction identifiers. Payment card details or UPI credentials are handled securely by certified payment gateways (PhonePe, Razorpay); we do not store full card numbers or banking passwords.
  • Device & Notification Tokens: Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM) device tokens to deliver call invitations, queue alerts, and consultation updates.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To authenticate your login via secure SMS OTP.
  • To generate accurate astronomical charts, planetary dasa tables, and horoscope predictions.
  • To facilitate real-time peer-to-peer audio and video consultations with astrologers.
  • To maintain transparent wallet accounting and per-minute consultation deductions.
  • To provide customer support and resolve technical or call quality inquiries.

3. Data Sharing & Third-Party Processors

We only share data with essential infrastructure partners under strict confidentiality:

  • Payment Gateways: PhonePe & Razorpay for processing wallet recharges securely.
  • SMS Gateways: MSG91 for delivering time-sensitive authentication OTPs.
  • Push Notifications: Google Firebase for delivering instant service notifications.

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal or birth details to third-party marketing networks.

4. Data Retention & User Rights

Your profile data and consultation history are retained as long as your account remains active. You possess the following rights regarding your personal information:

  • Access & Correction: You can review and update your name and birth details through your profile screen.
  • Account & Data Deletion: You can submit a permanent account deletion request anytime by visiting our dedicated Account Deletion Page. Upon processing, your profile, consultation history, and associated records will be permanently removed.

5. Security Safeguards

All data transmitted between your browser or mobile application and our servers is encrypted using standard HTTPS/TLS protocols. Real-time media streams utilize encrypted WebRTC protocols.
